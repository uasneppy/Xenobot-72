## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** to make my group's automatic punishments timed, escalate repeat offenders step by step and let old warns
expire, **so that** a first slip costs less than a pattern of abuse, while a group that sets none of this keeps every reply and punishment
exactly as it is today.

This plan lets the owner turn warn expiry on from the Warnings page.

<objective>
Add the expiry control to the Warnings page: a line, a button, a preset screen, and one apply function beside the warn commands.

Purpose: TUNE-03's "With warn expiry set to N days" needs a door the owner can use. No command exists (D-38), so drift and authority tests
stand in for a parity test.
Output: `applyWarnExpiry`, the page's expiry line, button and screen with the `ne` and `nx` actions, and their tests.

Test time budget (D-45): about 2 s of the phase's 35 s.
</objective>

  and for the bot as an administrator. Opening the screen needs only the live admin check.
- The hint states what an owner needs to know: an expired warn stops counting at once, it is dropped from the member's record at their next
  warn change, and raising the expiry later never brings back a dropped warn (P-17). This is one plain sentence in each language.
- A press of the stored value writes the same row again and shows no notice, like the other Warnings controls.
- The Warnings page is not `PrivateOnly`, so the expiry screen also works in a menu opened in the group (P-21).
