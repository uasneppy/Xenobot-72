## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** to make my group's automatic punishments timed, escalate repeat offenders step by step and let old warns
expire, **so that** a first slip costs less than a pattern of abuse, while a group that sets none of this keeps every reply and punishment
exactly as it is today.

This plan makes the blacklist's punishment timed, by command and from the Blacklist page. With it, the timed actions of TUNE-01 are complete.

<objective>
Let the blacklist apply a timed mute or a timed ban with a duration, set by `/blacklistaction tmute|tban <duration>` and by the Blacklist action
screen, for every word at once, through one apply function. Move the watcher's action switch into one helper, and make the shared
blacklist/forward-blocklist notice take an `autoPunishment`.

Purpose: TUNE-01 for the blacklist (success criterion 1).
Output: the blacklist repository writes, the command form, the watcher's timed punishment and notice, the action screen's timed row, and their
tests.

Test time budget (D-45): about 3 s of the phase's 35 s.
</objective>
