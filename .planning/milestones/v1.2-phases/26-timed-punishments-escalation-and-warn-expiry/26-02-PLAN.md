## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** to make my group's automatic punishments timed, escalate repeat offenders step by step and let old warns
expire, **so that** a first slip costs less than a pattern of abuse, while a group that sets none of this keeps every reply and punishment
exactly as it is today.

This plan is the tracer. It takes one timed punishment, the warn limit's timed mute or ban, through every layer the timed actions touch: the
migration, the repository, the shared timed helpers, the apply function, the command, the punishment request, the event message, the private
copy and the member requirement warn path.

<objective>
Make the warn limit apply a timed mute or a timed ban with a duration set by `/setwarnmode tmute <duration>` or
`/setwarnmode tban <duration>`, end to end. Along the way, add the migration of every duration column and the shared timed-punishment core
that plans 26-03 to 26-05 and 26-09 reuse.

Purpose: prove the architecture of TUNE-01 (one apply function, an end date computed once right before the request, an inert setting with no
duration, the event placeholders filled) on one rule before the other two rules and the pages copy it.
Output: one migration, the core file `punish_timed.go`, the warn changes, the engine's timed values, the default texts, the private copy's
Until line, and their tests.

Test time budget (D-45): about 3 s of the phase's 35 s in `fuku/modules` (one environment per subtest, real locales only where a default text
is compared).
</objective>
