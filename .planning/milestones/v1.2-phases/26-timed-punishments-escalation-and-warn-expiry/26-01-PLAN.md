## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** to make my group's automatic punishments timed, escalate repeat offenders step by step and let old warns
expire, **so that** a first slip costs less than a pattern of abuse, while a group that sets none of this keeps every reply and punishment
exactly as it is today.

This plan is the phase's first commit. It freezes every command reply, send site and export that the phase touches and that no golden pins yet.
Every later plan can then prove "no change" for a group that sets nothing.

<objective>
Record on the unchanged code the characterization goldens of every command and send site this phase changes that no existing golden pins
(MSG-11, D-02, D-44), and commit them alone before any production change. The existing goldens already pin the warn, warn limit, flood,
blacklist, forward blocklist and requirement notices, `/setwarnmode`, `/setwarnlimit` and the blacklist commands. This plan adds:
- the antiflood commands;
- the warn display and removal commands;
- the bare timed words of the three commands;
- the inert timed settings at every send site;
- the backup export of the three modules.

Purpose: success criterion 4 ("a group that sets none of this behaves exactly as today, and every argument the three commands accept today
answers as before") is provable only against goldens recorded first.
Output: two new test files in one commit.

Test time budget (D-45): about 4 s of the phase's 35 s in `fuku/modules`, plus the backup golden in `fuku/db/backup`. One environment per case,
real locales (uk), no language sweep.
</objective>
