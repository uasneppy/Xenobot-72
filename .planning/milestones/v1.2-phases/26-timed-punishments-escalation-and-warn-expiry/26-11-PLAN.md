## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** to make my group's automatic punishments timed, escalate repeat offenders step by step and let old warns
expire, **so that** a first slip costs less than a pattern of abuse, while a group that sets none of this keeps every reply and punishment
exactly as it is today.

This plan holds the whole phase to the repo's safety and privacy rules in one sweep, after every feature exists.

<objective>
Add the cross-cutting safety tests of Phase 26:
- logs that carry IDs only;
- texts that fit every language;
- codes and forged presses;
- reads that create nothing;
- the cost of the new paths;
- the Staff Group privacy sweep over every new screen.

Then reconcile AGENTS.md with the code.

Purpose: the per-feature plans tested their own behaviour. This plan proves the phase as a whole leaks nothing and costs nothing a group did
not ask for.
Output: two new test files, two extended ones, and the AGENTS.md read-through.

Test time budget (D-45): about 3 s of the phase's 35 s (language sweeps over pure renders only; one environment per dispatcher subtest).
</objective>
