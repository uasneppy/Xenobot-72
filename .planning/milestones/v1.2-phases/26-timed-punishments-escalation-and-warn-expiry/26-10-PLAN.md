## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** to make my group's automatic punishments timed, escalate repeat offenders step by step and let old warns
expire, **so that** a first slip costs less than a pattern of abuse, while a group that sets none of this keeps every reply and punishment
exactly as it is today.

This plan gives the owner the page that turns the ladder on and defines its steps.

<objective>
Add the private Escalation page (key `j`) with its step screen, and add the apply functions behind its controls.

Purpose: TUNE-02 says "the owner defines what the first, second and later automatic offences of one member get, per group, off by default".
This plan is that door. No command exists (D-32), so drift and authority tests stand in for parity tests.
Output: `escalation_settings.go`, `settings_escalation.go`, the Main grid update, the strings, and their tests.

Test time budget (D-45): about 3 s of the phase's 35 s (language sweeps over pure renders, one environment per dispatcher subtest).
</objective>
