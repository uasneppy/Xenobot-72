## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** to set what the bot posts in my group when a lockdown starts and when it is lifted, **so that** these messages sound like
my community, while the technical notes, the alerts and every raid-scale path stay exactly as they are and a group that sets nothing sees no change.

<objective>
Route `raidPostNotice` through the lockdown adapter: the trigger's event (`lockjoin`, `lockflood`, `lockai`), the group's language, no actor ("an admin"),
the bot as the person, the trigger's numbers and sentence as values, the technical notes after a custom text, `raidSend` (today's call) as the default and
a custom message sent inside one paced unit of the lockdown pacer, never queued for deletion. The call keeps running inside `raidPostAsync`, so the
`StopRaidPosts` drain covers it with no change. The cannot-lock warning, which shares the file and `raidSend`, is untouched.

Purpose: success criterion 2 (EVT-07); the goldens of plan 25-01 prove nothing changes for a group with nothing stored.
Output: the automatic site, its tests, the guard entry and the AGENTS.md lines.

Test time budget (D-39): about 3 s of the phase's 28 s. `raidFire` called directly for the three kinds (one environment per subtest), one real join surge
through the dispatcher, the drain proved with a 20 ms wait.
</objective>
