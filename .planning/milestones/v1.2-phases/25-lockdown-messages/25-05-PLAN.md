## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** to set what the bot posts in my group when a lockdown starts and when it is lifted, **so that** these messages sound like
my community, while the technical notes, the alerts and every raid-scale path stay exactly as they are and a group that sets nothing sees no change.

<objective>
Route the lift notice of every door through the lockdown adapter: `/unlockdown`'s reply for a recorded lift (`postLockdownLiftReplyEvent`, today's
`msg.Reply` as the default), `lockdownAnnounceLift` for the alert's Lift Confirm and a private menu (`postLockdownLiftEvent`, today's paced notice as the
default, the worker woken first), and a group menu's Lift Yes (`postLockdownLiftEvent` with no default: only a stored custom message is posted, D-22). The
lift's facts (a manual change, joiners being unbanned) follow a custom text. Every other answer of `/unlockdown`, the alert card's edits, the menu's redraw
and the tally are untouched.

Purpose: success criterion 3 (EVT-08); the goldens of plan 25-01 prove nothing changes for a group with nothing stored.
Output: the two lift sites, the three call sites, the tests, the guard entries and the AGENTS.md lines.

Test time budget (D-39): about 3 s of the phase's 28 s. One environment per subtest (a lockdown environment for the command and the alert, a settings
environment for the menus), no language sweep.
</objective>
