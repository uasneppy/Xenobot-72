## Provisional Owner Decisions Implemented Here

- **P29-01 (Full Mirror on Copy):** Copying overwrites the target's messages and tuning in scope to match the source completely. An empty/default source entry clears the target's custom message, switches/options, escalation ladder, manual defaults, reason shortcuts, or advanced thresholds.
- **P29-03 (Blacklist Action Without Copying Words):** Blacklist words themselves are never copied, added, or deleted. Only the source's blacklist action & timed duration (from the source's first blacklist row, or `"warn", 0` if the source has no blacklist words) are applied to the target's existing blacklist rows (if any) via `blacklists.SetBlacklistActionTimedContext`.
- **P29-04 (Warns & Antiflood Tuning Scope):** Copies `warns_settings.{warn_mode, warn_mode_secs, warn_expiry_days}` (preserving target's `warn_limit` or default 3, never touching `warns_users`) and `antiflood_settings.{action, action_secs}` (preserving target's `flood_limit` and `delete_antiflood_message`, and inserting `flood_limit = 0` when the target has no row so antiflood is never accidentally turned on).
