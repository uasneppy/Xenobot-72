## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** to customize the message for a failed captcha and the notices for member requirement breaches, and to turn on a
rate-limited notice for AI spam deletions, **so that** these messages sound like my community, while a group that sets nothing sees no change.

This plan is the phase's first commit: it freezes today's notices so every later plan can prove "no change" for a group with nothing stored.

production change: the captcha failure notice, the four requirement notices and the warn-limit variants, the requirement pause notice (it shares
`memberReqPostNotice`, which plan 24-05 changes), aispam's breaker notice and its silent delete branch (`aispam.go` changes in plan 24-06).

Purpose: without these goldens no later plan can prove that a group with no custom message keeps byte-identical notices and that the AI filter still
posts nothing in the group.

</objective>
