## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** to customize the message for a failed captcha and the notices for member requirement breaches, and to turn on a
rate-limited notice for AI spam deletions, **so that** these messages sound like my community, while a group that sets nothing sees no change.

This plan switches the requirement notices to the engine: the warn, kick and ban notices first, then the mute notice with its "I fixed it" button.

<objective>
Route `memberReqAnnounce`'s notice through `postEventSent` with a requirement site that sends both the custom message and the default through the
requirement pacer and its retry loop, schedules through `memberReqScheduleNotice` (the 24 hour cap for a mute), keeps the "I fixed it" row below the custom
buttons, ignores a stored Silent and a stored media item on the mute notice, and appends the requirements line and the remedies after a custom text; keep the
pause notice on `memberReqPostNotice`, now a wrapper of the same retry loop.

Purpose: success criteria 2 and 4 for the requirement notices; the goldens of plan 24-01 prove nothing changes for a group with nothing stored.
Output: the requirement site, its tests, the guard and the AGENTS.md lines.

Test time budget (D-40): about 4 s of the phase's 30 s. One requirement environment per subtest, the last-name rule only (the remedies are proved purely in
plan 24-02), no language sweep.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| member's profile to the notice | the member's names (which may carry bidirectional controls) reach a custom text |
| owner's stored text to a requirement notice | the owner's text is posted after an automatic punishment, before the bot's own requirements line |
| notice to the member's way out | the mute notice carries the only self-service way out of the mute |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-24-15 | Denial of service | a custom message, a Silent row or a stored media item leaving a muted member with no "I fixed it" button | high | mitigate | `KeepRows` with `KeepRowsLast`, `Required` and `TextOnly` set by the site, never by the stored row; `custom keeps the button` and `silent and media are ignored` subtests |
| T-24-16 | Tampering | a member's name with bidirectional controls reordering a custom requirement text | medium | mitigate | the site passes the names through `memberReqStripBidi` and the name cap (D-15); plan 24-07's privacy and log tests use such a name |
| T-24-17 | Denial of service | a rate-limited custom mute notice counted as undelivered, lifting the mute | high | mitigate | the custom send goes through the same loop as the default (waits out `ErrRateLimited` within the mute budget); only a refusal falls back to the default, and only both failing lifts the mute (`undelivered` subtest) |
| T-24-18 | Repudiation | a punishment with no notice because a custom message was refused | medium | mitigate | the engine's refusal fallback sends the default (`refused` subtest) |
| T-24-19 | Spoofing | a tap rescheduling or editing a message that is not the hold's notice | medium | mitigate | `memberreq_fixed.go` is unchanged; the stored notice ID is the posted message's; `TestMemberReqFixedReschedulesOnlyTheNotice` runs unchanged |
| T-24-SC | Tampering | package installs | low | accept | no dependency |
