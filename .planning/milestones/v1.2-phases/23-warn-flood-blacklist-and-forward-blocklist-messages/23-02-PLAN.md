## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** to customize what the bot says when it warns someone, when a member reaches the warn limit, and when flood, a
blacklisted word or a blocked forward gets punished, in the group and by private message, **so that** these messages sound like my community,
while a group that sets nothing sees no change.

This plan is the phase's tracer and its engine extension: the warn message goes end to end through a generic engine first, then the rest of the
engine's vocabulary (event keys, action words, the flood reason and the default builders) is added for the plans that follow.

<objective>
Make the event message engine generic and prove it end to end on the warn message: a site that is not the moderation pipeline (warnThisUser)
posts a custom message, keeps the bot's Remove warn row after the custom buttons and falls back to the default; then add the five event keys, the
translated action words and the flood reason, and the four default builders the other sites and the Messages page will use, each proven byte for
byte against the goldens of plan 23-01 before any other site is switched.

Purpose: the engine of Phase 21 is a method of the moderation pipeline; automatic punishments have no moderation context. The riskiest change of
the phase (the engine's generalisation under the manual goldens) is made and proven first.
Output: the generic engine, the warn site, the Automatic family's builders and words, their tests and the AGENTS.md lines.
</objective>

  `ReplyParameters{MessageId: msg.MessageId, AllowSendingWithoutReply: true}` added, so the request is today's byte for byte. The limit branch keeps
  today's send and scheduling in this plan (plan 23-04 switches it).
- The goldens of plan 23-01 are JSON requests with the token `<chat>`; `TestAutoEventDefaults` replaces the token with `0`, decodes each golden and
