## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** to customize what the bot says when it warns someone, when a member reaches the warn limit, and when flood, a
blacklisted word or a blocked forward gets punished, in the group and by private message, **so that** these messages sound like my community,
while a group that sets nothing sees no change.

This plan proves the five success criteria of the phase end to end and closes its two requirements.
