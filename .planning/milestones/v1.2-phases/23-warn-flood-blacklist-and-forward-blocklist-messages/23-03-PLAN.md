## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** to customize what the bot says when it warns someone, when a member reaches the warn limit, and when flood, a
blacklisted word or a blocked forward gets punished, in the group and by private message, **so that** these messages sound like my community,
while a group that sets nothing sees no change.

This plan puts the five events on the Messages page: the owner can set, preview and silence each of them from the group's settings in the bot's
private chat.

<objective>
Add the Automatic family to the Messages page as table rows (D-24): five events with their codes, icons, names, descriptions, reserved bot
buttons, default builders and sample values; its strings in all 8 languages; the sample-filled previews; and the expected shapes of the page's
existing tests (the hub gains a family cell). The editor definitions come from the existing loop over the families, so the editor, the typed-reply
door, Set, Remove, Silent, the previews and the private copy screens work for the new events with no engine change.

Purpose: success criterion 1 (the family with five events, each opening the editor with a group message and a Silent switch); the private copy of
each event appears on its screen once plan 23-05 registers its spec.
Output: the family, its strings, its tests and the AGENTS.md lines.
