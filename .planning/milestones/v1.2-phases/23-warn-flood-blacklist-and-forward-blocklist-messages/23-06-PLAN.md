## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** to customize what the bot says when it warns someone, when a member reaches the warn limit, and when flood, a
blacklisted word or a blocked forward gets punished, in the group and by private message, **so that** these messages sound like my community,
while a group that sets nothing sees no change.

This plan holds the phase to the settings menu's and the engine's safety rules: forged presses, rights, reads that create nothing, sizes, logs and
privacy.

<objective>
Extend the Messages page's safety tests and the engine's log and privacy tests to the Automatic family and its five sends, and add the two tests the new
sites need: one that no log line carries content, one that the forward blocklist never names the source or the Staff Group anywhere. A production fix is
made only when a test finds a defect, in the file it names, and is recorded in the SUMMARY.

Purpose: the page's and the engine's rules hold for the new events (MSG-08, D-26, D-31, D-32) with the light tests the package's time budget allows.
Output: extended safety tests, two new tests and the AGENTS.md lines.
</objective>
