## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** to customize what the bot says when it warns someone, when a member reaches the warn limit, and when flood, a
blacklisted word or a blocked forward gets punished, in the group and by private message, **so that** these messages sound like my community,
while a group that sets nothing sees no change.

This plan gives the five events their private copies under Phase 22's rules, the per-group cap of automatic events included.

<objective>
Register a private copy spec for each of the five events (warn and warn limit manual, flood, blacklist and forward blocklist automatic), let a reply
raised by an automatic rule count against the cap even when its event's spec is manual, pass the count, the limit and the action to the default private
texts, and add those five texts in all 8 languages; then prove through the real sites that each copy is sent after the punishment and the group message,
that the cap bounds a flood of offenders while every punishment happens, and that the page shows each event's private copy.

Purpose: success criterion 5 and the private half of success criterion 1.
Output: five specs, five default texts, the engine's per-reply cap flag, their tests and the AGENTS.md lines.
</objective>
