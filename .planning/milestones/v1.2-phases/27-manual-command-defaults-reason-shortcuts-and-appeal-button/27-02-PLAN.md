## Provisional Owner Decisions Recorded Here (Easy to Confirm or Reverse)

- **P27-01 (Default durations on silent & delete-first variants):** When `default_ban_secs > 0`, `/ban` (user target, not channel ban), `/dban`, and `/sban` all apply `UntilDate` computed from `default_ban_secs`; `/ban` and `/dban` emit `EventTimedBan` with `Duration` and `Until` so both default and custom messages show the duration, while `/sban` stays silent. Symmetrically, when `default_mute_secs > 0`, `/mute`, `/dmute`, and `/smute` apply `default_mute_secs`, with `/mute` and `/dmute` emitting `EventTimedMute`. Explicit `/tban` and `/tmute` always keep their explicit duration (and still require a duration token as pinned in `27-01`).
- **P27-02 (Commands covered by `reason_required`):** Covers all manual user-punishment commands: `/ban` (user target only; channel `/ban` exempt since it has no reason line), `/dban`, `/sban`, `/tban`, `/kick`, `/dkick`, `/skick`, `/mute`, `/dmute`, `/smute`, `/tmute`, `/warn`, `/dwarn`, `/swarn`. For `/tban` and `/tmute`, the check runs AFTER duration extraction so a bare `/tban 2h` is refused while `/tban 2h spam` succeeds. For `/dkick`, `/sban`, `/skick`, `/smute`, trailing text after the target is extracted as optional reason (with zero change when omitted, preserving all goldens). `/unban`, `/unmute`, `/kickme`, automatic rules (`warnSourceAuto`), and Staff Group commands are never affected.
