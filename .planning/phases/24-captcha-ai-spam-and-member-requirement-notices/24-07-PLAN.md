## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** to customize the message for a failed captcha and the notices for member requirement breaches, and to turn on a
rate-limited notice for AI spam deletions, **so that** these messages sound like my community, while a group that sets nothing sees no change.

This plan holds the phase to the settings menu's and the engine's safety rules: forged presses, rights, reads that create nothing, cost, sizes, logs and
privacy.

<objective>
Extend the Messages page's safety tests and the engine's log and privacy tests to the six new events and their three sites, and add the two tests the new
sites need: one that no log line carries content, one that no group message, private copy, Details record or log line names the Staff Group or reveals the AI
verdict. A production fix is made only when a test finds a defect, in the file it names, and is recorded in the SUMMARY.

Purpose: the page's and the engine's rules hold for the new events (MSG-08, D-32, D-36, D-37) with the light tests the package's time budget allows.
Output: extended safety tests, three new tests and the AGENTS.md lines.

Test time budget (D-40): about 5 s of the phase's 30 s. One environment per test where a dispatcher is needed; the byte and length checks stay in the pure
renders of `settingsMsgScreens`; the privacy test runs one site per surface, not a cross product.
</objective>

| Boundary | Description |

| callback data to the page | attacker-controlled fields from any button press |
| bot to its logs | the logs leave the process and are read by operators |
