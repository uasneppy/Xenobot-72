## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** to customize the message for a failed captcha and the notices for member requirement breaches, and to turn on a
rate-limited notice for AI spam deletions, **so that** these messages sound like my community, while a group that sets nothing sees no change.

This plan gives the six new events their private copies under Phase 22's rules, before the Messages page shows the events (plan 24-04), so every
event screen offers its private copy from its first appearance.

<objective>
Register a private copy spec for each of the six events, all automatic (D-34), add their six default private texts in all 8 languages, and prove through
the captcha site (the only new site already switched, plan 24-02) that the copy is sent after the action and the group notice and that the per-group cap
bounds a burst while every member is still punished. The requirement and AI spam copies are proved by their own site plans (24-05 and 24-06), which
switch those sites to the engine.

Purpose: the private half of success criterion 1 for the captcha and the spec table every later plan relies on.
Output: six specs, six default texts, their tests and the AGENTS.md lines.

Test time budget (D-40): about 3 s of the phase's 30 s. One site per subtest, uk only, the cap proved with three members, not ten.
</objective>
