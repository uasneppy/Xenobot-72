## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** to customize the message for a failed captcha and the notices for member requirement breaches, and to turn on a
rate-limited notice for AI spam deletions, **so that** these messages sound like my community, while a group that sets nothing sees no change.

This plan puts the six events on the Messages page: the owner can set, preview and silence each of them, and turn the AI notice on, from the group's
settings in the bot's private chat.

<objective>
Add the captcha and AI spam events to the Automatic family and the Requirements family with its four events as table rows (D-28), with their strings in
all 8 languages; give the editor a text reserve (`ReservedText`) so a requirement text plus the bot's requirements line always fits; draw the AI spam
event's switch as a Notice switch that is off by default; keep the requirement mute event unsilenceable; fill the previews with sample values and the
requirement note; and update the expected shapes of the page's existing tests. The editor definitions still come from the existing loop over the
families, so Set, Remove, the typed-reply door, the previews and the private copy screens work for the new events with no new action code.

Purpose: success criterion 1's page side for the captcha, the page side of success criteria 2 and 3.
Output: the table rows, the editor reserve, the Notice switch, the strings, the tests and the AGENTS.md lines.

Test time budget (D-40): about 3 s of the phase's 30 s. One environment per dispatcher subtest; the reserve's check is a pure test; language sweeps stay in
the existing pure renders of `settingsMsgScreens`.
