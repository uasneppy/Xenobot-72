## Phase Goal

This plan wires TUNE-05 (reason shortcut validation, apply functions, and expansion at manual reason parsing) across all manual punishment commands so `/ban @user spam`, `/tban @user 2h spam`, `/mute @user spam`, `/kick @user spam`, and `/warn @user spam` expand shortcuts everywhere the reason is displayed or logged.

<objective>
Implement the reason-shortcut validation/apply helpers and wire single-word shortcut expansion into manual punishment reason parsing (`moderation.go`, `bans.go`, `mute.go`, `warns.go:dispatchWarn`), proving expansion in group messages, PM copies, warn records, and log channel posts with HTML escaping and zero leakage into automatic or Staff Group paths.

Purpose: deliver the runtime and apply layer of TUNE-05 ahead of the Reason Shortcuts list page (Plan 27-06).
Output: shortcut apply & expansion helpers in `modtuning_settings.go`, wiring in manual command handlers, and comprehensive tests in `modtuning_reasons_test.go`.
</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-27-06 | Tampering / Injection (HTML injection) | `expandManualReason` output rendered into group replies, custom `{reason}`, PM copies, and log posts | high | mitigate | Store raw plain text and rely on HTML escaping at every render site (`moderationReasonLine`, `eventPlaceholderValues`, `eventPMDefaultText`, `warnDefaultReply`, `warnLimitDefaultReply`, `actionlog.Admin`); `TestReasonShortcutExpansion` explicitly tests `<promo> &` across all four surfaces. |
| T-27-07 | Denial of Service | Unbounded shortcut count or huge shortcut text breaking Telegram 4096/1024 limits or DB size | medium | mitigate | Cap at 50 shortcuts per group (`ReasonShortcutsCapMax`), word <= 32 runes, full text <= 500 runes (enforced in both Go validation and DB CHECK), and fast-path skip DB lookup on empty/multi-word reasons. |
| T-27-08 | Information Disclosure | Shortcut word/reason written to application logs | low | mitigate | All `modtuning_settings.go` log lines record `chatID`, `userID`, and error class only; verified by log capture in `TestReasonShortcutValidationAndApply`. |
| T-27-SC | Tampering | Go module dependencies | low | accept | No new dependency added. |
