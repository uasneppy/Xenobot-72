<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Untrusted `/unroll*` Arguments → Redis Log Query | Arbitrary integers/durations could cause unbounded scans or pull cross-topic messages if bounds and `Thread` filters are missing. |
| `MessageReactionUpdated` (`✍`) → Private Brief & AI Budget | Anonymous actors, Staff Groups, locked-down chats, non-permitted audiences, or reaction tapping could leak data or drain `brief:b` hourly budgets. |
| PM Delivery Failure (`403`) on `silentPM: true` | Group-facing error/typing notices on `✍` PM failure would spam the group for every user who never `/start`ed the bot. |
