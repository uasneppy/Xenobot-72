## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** to make my group's automatic punishments timed, escalate repeat offenders step by step and let old warns
expire, **so that** a first slip costs less than a pattern of abuse, while a group that sets none of this keeps every reply and punishment
exactly as it is today.

This plan gives the timed warn limit its page and builds the duration screen that every later timed control reuses.

<objective>
Add Timed mute and Timed ban to the Warnings page through one shared duration screen, writing through `applyWarnMode`, the function
`/setwarnmode` calls. Also make the Messages page's previews and prompt cover `{duration}` and `{until}` for the automatic events.

Purpose: success criterion 1 says "from the page and by command"; this plan is the page half for the warn limit, and the screen it adds is
the component plans 26-04, 26-05 and 26-10 reuse.
Output: `settings_duration.go`, the Warnings page's timed row and two page actions, the Messages page sample and prompt, and their tests.

Test time budget (D-45): about 3 s of the phase's 35 s (language sweeps over pure renders only, one environment per dispatcher subtest).
</objective>

```go
// renderSettingsDuration draws the duration screen: the page header, the intro (settings_duration_intro_tmute or _tban),
// the hint, the presets two to a row (punishDurationLabel, settingsSelectedPrefix on selected), then Back (backFields)
// and Close. press builds the callback fields of one preset. It is pure.
func renderSettingsDuration(v settingsView, titleKey, action string, selected int,
	press func(secs int) map[string]string, backFields map[string]string, notice string) settingsScreen
// settingsTimedWord is the word of an effective timed action on every page (Warnings, Antiflood, Blacklist, Escalation):
// settings_opt_timed with the settings_opt_tmute or _tban word and punishDurationLabel; ok is false for anything else,
// so the caller keeps its own word for an untimed or inert action.
func settingsTimedWord(tr *i18n.Translator, action string, secs int) (word string, ok bool)
```
