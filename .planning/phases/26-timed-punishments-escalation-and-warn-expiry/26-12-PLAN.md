## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** to make my group's automatic punishments timed, escalate repeat offenders step by step and let old warns
expire, **so that** a first slip costs less than a pattern of abuse, while a group that sets none of this keeps every reply and punishment
exactly as it is today.

This plan proves the phase end to end, as the owner and the members would use it, and runs the phase's gates.

<objective>
Write `TestTimedPunishmentsEndToEnd`, which drives every success criterion of Phase 26 through the real dispatcher with real button presses of
the drawn pages. Then run the full gates and record the full `fuku/modules` time.

Purpose: the per-feature tests prove the pieces; this proves the owner's flows and the "nothing set, nothing changes" promise together.
Output: one end-to-end test file, the final AGENTS.md Testing entry and the gate results in the SUMMARY.

Test time budget (D-45): about 4 s of the phase's 35 s; the full-package run is measured once and recorded.
</objective>
