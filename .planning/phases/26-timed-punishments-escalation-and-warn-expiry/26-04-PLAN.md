## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** to make my group's automatic punishments timed, escalate repeat offenders step by step and let old warns
expire, **so that** a first slip costs less than a pattern of abuse, while a group that sets none of this keeps every reply and punishment
exactly as it is today.

This plan makes the flood's punishment timed, by command and from the Antiflood page.

<objective>
Let antiflood apply a timed mute or a timed ban with a duration, set by `/setfloodmode tmute|tban <duration>` and by the Antiflood page's
Timed row, through one apply function. Move the flood's action switch into one helper, so the ladder (plan 26-09) has one place to change.

Purpose: TUNE-01 for antiflood (success criterion 1).
Output: the antiflood repository write and read, the command forms, the flood's timed punishment and notice, the page row, and their tests.

Test time budget (D-45): about 3 s of the phase's 35 s.
</objective>
