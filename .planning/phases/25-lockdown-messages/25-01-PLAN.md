## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** to set what the bot posts in my group when a lockdown starts (by an admin or by the join surge, flood or AI burst
trigger) and when it is lifted, **so that** these messages sound like my community, while the technical notes, the alerts and every raid-scale path
stay exactly as they are and a group that sets nothing sees no change.

This plan is the phase's first commit: it freezes today's lockdown and lift texts so every later plan can prove "no change" for a group with nothing
stored.

<objective>
Record the characterization golden of every send site this phase changes (MSG-11, D-06, D-38) on the unchanged code and commit it alone, before any
production change: the `/lockdown` success reply, the three automatic notices, the cannot-lock warning (it shares `raid_trigger.go` and `raidSend` with
the automatic notice), and the lift notice from every door, with the group menu's lift pinned as sending nothing.

Purpose: without these goldens no later plan can prove that a group with no custom message keeps byte-identical lockdown texts.
Output: one new test file, committed alone.

Test time budget (D-39): about 3 s of the phase's 28 s. One environment per case, no language sweep, the real locales only (Ukrainian, the default
group language).
</objective>

  rights come from `env.fake.setBotMember(env.chat.Id, lockdownBotAdminJSON(canRestrict, canDelete, canInvite))`. The anonymous case uses
  `lockdown_anon_test.go`'s `sendAnonymous("/lockdown")` and `tapProof(tapper)` with a fresh tapper who is a live administrator with the restrict right;
  the pinned request is the success reply that follows the tap (the proof prompt and its deletion are not part of the golden).
- The automatic notices: `raidFire(env.bot, &env.chat, raidTrigger{...})` called directly (the path `raid_notice_test.go` and `raid_ai_burst_test.go` use)
  with `{Kind: models.LockdownTriggerJoinSurge, Count: 15, Window: 60}`, `{Kind: models.LockdownTriggerFlood, Count: 30, Window: 30, Percent: 80}` and
  `{Kind: models.LockdownTriggerAIBurst, Count: 5, Window: 120}`, then `raidPosts.wait()` before reading; `raidCleanup` for the raid rows of the chat.
- The cannot-lock warning: the bot without the restrict right, a Staff Group linked with `linkLockdownStaffGroup`, a log channel set with
  `setStaffLogChannel` (admin category on), `raidFire` with the join surge trigger; the two copies are pinned with `<staff>` and `<log>`.
- The lifts: `/unlockdown` after `env.lockAsAdmin("raid")`; for the unbanning case two joiner rows are seeded banned with `seedLockdownJoiner` and the live
  permissions changed with `env.fake.setChatPermsRaw` before the lift, so both notes appear. The alert lift uses `askToLiftLockdown` and
  `pressLockdownButton` with the card's Confirm (`lockdown_alert_lift_test.go`). The menu lifts use `newSettingsEnvWith(t, settingsEnvOptions{RealLocale:
  true})`, `lockAsAdmin`, and the presses of `TestSettingsLockdownLiftPrivate` and `TestSettingsLockdownLift` (`settings_lockdown_lift_test.go`).
- Message IDs and dates are deterministic within one environment (the fake numbers them), so only chat and user IDs are tokens. Texts are recorded in the
  group's default language (uk).
