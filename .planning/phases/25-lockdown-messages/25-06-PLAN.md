## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** to set what the bot posts in my group when a lockdown starts and when it is lifted, **so that** these messages sound like
my community, while the technical notes, the alerts and every raid-scale path stay exactly as they are and a group that sets nothing sees no change.

<objective>
Prove EVT-10 behaviourally: with every lockdown message stored, every surface that is not a lockdown event (the alert and its reminders and close, the
raid-scale removals and their reports, the lift tally, the cannot-lock warning, Staff Group actions, the captcha challenge and log posts) sends exactly what
it sends today and never picks up a lockdown message; and prove that no lockdown event ever sends a private copy (D-04, D-35).

Purpose: success criterion 4 (EVT-10) as observed behaviour, beside the structural guard of plan 25-02.
Output: one new test file, one extended spec test and the AGENTS.md lines.

Test time budget (D-39): about 4 s of the phase's 28 s. One environment per surface (the lockdown flow shares one environment), no language sweep, the sentinel
search over the recorded requests.
</objective>

## Planner assumptions (flagged)

- The lockdown flow runs in one environment built like `banEnv` (`lockdown_ban_test.go`: a lockdown environment with a Staff Group and a burst) plus a log
  channel set with `setStaffLogChannel` (admin category on), so one flow reaches every lockdown surface: `/lockdown`; joins recorded during the lockdown; worker
  cycles that post the alert to the Staff Group and the log channel; a reminder made due with `ageAlert` (`lockdown_reminder_test.go`) and posted by a cycle;
  the alert's Ban button pressed through `askBan` and confirmed, then `cyclesUntilReported` so the run's card report and its `#LOCKDOWN_BAN` post are sent;
  the alert's Lift Confirm (`askToLiftLockdown`-style presses on the alert copy); further cycles that close the alert copies and post the tally.
- A surface is "unchanged" when no request to it carries either sentinel; the texts of the surfaces in this phase's touched files are also pinned by
  `TestLegacyLockdownMessageRequests` (the cannot-lock warning) and by the existing lockdown tests, which run unedited.
- The Staff Group action uses `newStaffActionEnv(t, 2)` and `runStaffCommand("/mute 4242 flooding")` (the pattern of
  `TestDeletionExcludedMessagesNeverScheduled`), the captcha challenge `SendCaptcha` on a `newBotSite` with the captcha on, the log post `actionlog.Admin`;
  each with every lockdown message stored for its chats.
- Private chats are positive chat IDs; the flows' actors (the admin, the tapper, the joiners) are positive users who must receive nothing.
